Non-Profit (NP) Membership

Cost: Free (No Fee for verified non-profits)

  • Basic listing in the online Chamber Directory
  • Invitation to monthly community networking mixers
  • Access to non-profit grant workshops

Bronze Membership

Cost: $50 / year

  • All Non-Profit Membership benefits
  • 10% discount on Chamber training seminars and workshops
  • Ribbon-cutting ceremony announcement in our newsletter

Silver Membership

Cost: $120 / year

  • All Bronze Membership benefits
  • Eligible for random spotlight rotation on the Chamber Home Page
  • 25% discount on Chamber events and trade fair booths

Gold Membership

Cost: $250 / year

  • All Silver Membership benefits
  • Priority Home Page spotlight placement and banner advertising
  • VIP access and complimentary tickets to annual Lekki Business Gala
  • Executive luncheons and dedicated business advocacy
